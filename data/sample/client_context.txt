Client: NovaCart
Industry: E-commerce marketplace
Primary users: CEO, VP Growth, Head of Category, Customer Experience Lead

Client business context:
NovaCart sells fashion, beauty, grocery, home, and electronics products across web, mobile, and marketplace channels. The company has grown quickly through acquisition-led campaigns, but leadership now wants more balanced growth across revenue quality, retention, fulfilment reliability, and category profitability.

Strategic priorities for this quarter:
- Identify which categories and channels are driving quality revenue.
- Separate high-revenue categories from categories creating service risk.
- Understand whether discounting is creating sustainable demand or masking weak conversion.
- Assess whether delivery delays and returns are harming customer experience.
- Build a concise CEO-ready narrative for the quarterly growth review.

Known client concerns from meetings:
- Electronics produces strong revenue but appears to have higher delivery delays and return risk.
- Mobile is growing, especially with loyal customers, but leadership wants confidence that this is not driven only by discounting.
- Grocery has frequent orders and high ratings, but lower order value.
- Fashion responds to discounts, but the team wants to understand whether it is margin-accretive.
- Leadership wants a board-style report, not a raw analytics dump.

Company product positioning:
InsightOS helps client-facing growth, product, and analytics teams convert messy context and operational data into decision-ready commercial intelligence. The platform combines agentic analysis, RAG over client knowledge, SQL/Python data analysis, chart generation, and executive report synthesis.

Standard product presentation guidance:
- Start with the client decision, not with the dataset.
- Use charts only when they support a recommendation.
- Label assumptions separately from evidence.
- Do not expose raw tables to LLM-facing agents.
- Show the DA-produced chart artifacts and SQL evidence in the appendix.
- End with prioritized actions, owners, and measurable next steps.

Sales and PM playbook:
- Position PM Agent as the client narrative owner.
- Position BA Agent as the business-to-data translator.
- Position DA Agent as the SQL/Python execution layer.
- Emphasize that raw tabular data remains inside deterministic analysis tooling.
- Show traceability from client question to SQL/Python output to final recommendation.
